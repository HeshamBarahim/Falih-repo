# Leaderboard Points System and Prizes

## How Points Are Counted
To rank on the leaderboard, users earn points based on their activities in the app. Here's the breakdown:

### Unit Exams:
- Completing a unit exam: **+2 points** (even if no correct answers).
- Each correct answer: **+1 point**.
- If all answers are correct: **+5 bonus points**.

### Final Exams:
- Completing a final exam: **+3 points** (even if no correct answers).
- Each correct answer: **+2 points**.
- If all answers are correct: **+15 bonus points**.

### Daily Login Streak:
- After logging in daily for a week: **+3 points per day**.
- For maintaining a 2-week streak with participation (taking at least one unit or final exam daily): **+15 bonus points**.

### Inviting Friends:
- For every friend who joins via your invitation: **+20 points**.

---

## Prizes for Top Performers
Prizes are awarded every **2 weeks** to the top 3 leaderboard participants:

### First Place:
- **40 Final Exams**.
- **30 Unit Exams**.

### Second Place:
- **30 Final Exams**.
- **20 Unit Exams**.

### Third Place:
- **20 Final Exams**.
- **10 Unit Exams**.
