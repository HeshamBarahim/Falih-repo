# Privacy Policy for Falih

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Falih! Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using Falih, you agree to the practices described here.

Questions? Contact us at **falihTeam@gmail.com**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Information you give us
- Full name and username
- Phone number
- School, governorate (the region you select), grade, department and gender
- A profile photo, if you choose to add one
- Your password. It is handled by Firebase Authentication and stored in protected form; we cannot read it.

### b. Information created while you use the app
- Your exam activity: answers, scores, points, time taken and dates
- Your ranking on the leaderboard
- Your notification history in the app
- A device token that lets us send you push notifications

### c. Technical information
- App version and device information needed to deliver the service and check for updates
- Your IP address, which our servers and our service providers process when you connect. We keep it only briefly, for abuse prevention (for example, limiting repeated verification requests), typically for no more than two days.

### d. What we do not collect
We do not access your device location, your contacts, or your email address. Falih uses an internal login identifier built from your phone number; it is not used to email you.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To create and secure your account, including verifying your phone number
- To provide the app: exams, results, progress, leaderboard and AI explanations
- To send notifications, updates and support messages
- To prevent abuse and keep the service secure
- To improve the app and fix problems

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 4. Verification Codes
When you sign up or reset your password, we send a one-time code to your phone number by **WhatsApp**. The code is checked on our servers, expires after 5 minutes, can be used once, and is allowed a limited number of attempts. We keep only a protected (hashed) form of the code, not the code itself. WhatsApp (Meta) processes the message to deliver it to you.

---

## 5. AI Assistance
When you ask for an explanation of a question, the app sends the question text, the answer choices, your selected answer and the correct answer to an AI service hosted on Amazon Web Services. We do not send your name, phone number or other account details with this request.

---

## 6. Sharing Your Information
- **Leaderboard:** your username, points and profile photo may be visible to other users of the app.
- **Service providers:** we use trusted providers to run Falih (see section 10). They process data only to provide their service to us.
- **Payments:** if you make a payment, the details you enter are used to process that transaction with the payment provider.
- **Legal compliance:** we may disclose information when required by law, regulation or legal process.

---

## 7. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions). Our providers' servers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. We protect your data with:

- **Encryption** in transit and at rest
- **Access rules** so that you can only read and change your own account data
- **Restricted access** for authorized personnel only
- **Secure APIs** for all data transfer

No system can guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong password and keep it private.

---

## 8. Retention and Deletion
We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app:

**Settings → Account → Delete account** (you will be asked to confirm your password).

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, exam history, notifications, profile photo and sign-in account. This cannot be undone. Some derived information, such as the monthly leaderboard, may continue to show your entry until it is next recalculated. If you cannot access the app, email us the phone number and username of the account; we may ask you to prove it is yours before we delete it.

---

## 9. Your Rights
- **Access:** you can ask for a copy of your data.
- **Correction:** you can update your profile in **Settings → Edit profile**, or ask us to correct it.
- **Deletion:** you can delete your account in the app, or ask us to.
- **Notifications:** you can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

To use these rights, contact us at **falihTeam@gmail.com**.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services
- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check): accounts, data storage, notifications and protection against abuse
- **WhatsApp (Meta):** delivery of verification codes
- **Amazon Web Services:** AI explanations
- **Payment providers:** processing payments

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

---

## 11. Children's Privacy
Falih is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

---

## 12. Updates to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes in the app. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
